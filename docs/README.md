# Prysma Launchpad

**Today, tokens are manipulated. We can fix it!**

Most token launches fall into the hands of snipers who get positioned early. Insider coordination then takes over, and attention is manufactured. Fake volumes, fake activity, fake hype. Finally, when unsuspecting retail arrives, they become the exit liquidity. This is single-handedly responsible for corrupting the market.

What if the same participants who are incentivized to extract from these markets are the ones incentivized to grow them? We believe that this is fundamentally about incentive alignment. We reward users for contributing to the market's distribution and growth. Prysma Launchpad combines auctions for price discovery with a growth mechanism that rewards those who bring demand to the auction.
