# How Does Distribution Work?

Prysma Launchpad currently runs **open auctions**. Anyone can bid. There is no invite gate.

Distributor NFTs (Recruits) still exist as a mintable collection. They are **not required to bid**, and they **do not currently earn trading fees**. Referral attribution and distributor fee share are planned for later.

## Getting Started

Prysma begins with an initial free mint of **1,000 Recruit NFTs**.

Holding a Recruit does not change who can participate in an auction.

## Distributor Tiers

Tiers and artwork still exist on the NFT. Volume-based upgrades and fee weight are **not live**.

| Tier           | Bid Volume | Weight |
| -------------- | ---------: | -----: |
| **Recruit**    |  < 0.5 ETH |     0× |
| **Scout**      |    0.5 ETH |     1× |
| **Promoter**   |      1 ETH |     2× |
| **Advocate**   |      5 ETH |    10× |
| **Ambassador** |     10 ETH |    20× |
| **Partner**    |     25 ETH |    35× |

## Rewards

After an auction graduates, trading generates hook fees. Those fees currently go to the **creator** (95%) and **platform** (5%). Distributors do not claim a share.
